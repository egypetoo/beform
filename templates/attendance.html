Clock in until 10:15 is allowed. From 10:16 to 11:00 is a quarter-day deduction. After 11:00 is a half-day deduction. If the employee clocked in on time, they get 15 minutes grace on clock-out (from 17:15). Leaving 16:30–17:14 is a quarter day; leaving before 16:30 is a half day. Working less than 8.5 hours after that grace is a half day. Missing punch in or punch out is a half-day deduction. Sales has no clock-out, so clock-out rules are skipped for that department. Late excuse is whole hours only and uses the 4-hour monthly allowance. No punch and no leave/mission request is a full day. Not working a Saturday in the month is a full-day deduction. Friday is skipped. Saturday is off unless the employee punched or submitted Monthly Saturday Work.
