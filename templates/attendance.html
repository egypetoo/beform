Clock-in is flexible from 9:00 to 10:00. The employee must complete 8.5 hours from that arrival (9:00 leaves at 17:30, 9:30 leaves at 18:00, 10:00 leaves at 18:30). 15 minutes of grace can be used on clock-in, clock-out, or split between both. From 10:16 to 11:00 is a quarter-day deduction. After 11:00 is a half-day deduction. If they still leave before completing 8.5 hours after grace: 7.5 hours or more worked is a quarter day, and less than 7.5 hours is a half day. Official holidays set by HR are not deducted. Missing punch in or punch out is a half-day deduction. Sales has no clock-out, so clock-out rules are skipped for that department. Late excuse is whole hours only and uses the 4-hour monthly allowance. No punch and no leave/mission request is a full day. Not working a Saturday in the month is a full-day deduction. Friday is skipped. Saturday is off unless the employee punched or submitted Monthly Saturday Work.
